# ProfNudge privacy policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

ProfNudge sends you reminders to review professors you chose to track. This page lists everything it collects.

## What the ProfNudge server stores

- **Your email address**, to send reminders and sign you in.
- **The professors you track**: their Rate My Professors ID, name and school (all public on Rate My Professors), the term, and whether you've reviewed, skipped or are still waiting.
- **Your term end dates**, to know when to remind you.
- **Whether you unsubscribed.**

## What never leaves your browser

- Your review text, scores, tags, grade and every other answer you type into the review panel.
- Your browsing history. ProfNudge only runs on ratemyprofessors.com pages.

## Who sees it

Only the ProfNudge maintainer's Supabase database (data storage) and Resend (email delivery). Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

## Deleting your data

Click **Delete my account** in the extension popup, or email the maintainer. Unsubscribing from emails stops all reminders immediately.
